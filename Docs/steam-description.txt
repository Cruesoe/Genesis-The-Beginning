[b]Adds "The Beginning", the Genesis starting scenario: two people with no memory and nothing to their name, alone in a world that's already old.[/b]

Intended to be played alongside the rest of the Genesis Mod Pack, but it works on its own.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]You start with two colonists aged 18 to 30, chosen from five.
[*]The colonists belong to the Wild Men faction, with its culture lock and classic mode.
[*]There is no starting equipment, no forced health conditions and no periodic wild man incident.
[*]Your colonists start without the ability to do basic tasks such as sowing, doctoring and hunting, so it is extremely difficult.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
It is a starting scenario, so start a new save to play it.
