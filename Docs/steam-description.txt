Adds "The Beginning", the Genesis starting scenario: two people with no memory and nothing to their name, alone in a world that's already old.

Built on Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals' Wild Men scenario - the same faction, ideo culture lock and classic mode - cut down to two starting colonists aged 18-30, with no starting equipment, no forced health conditions and no periodic wild man incident.

Intended to be played alongside the rest of the Genesis Mod Pack, but it works on its own.
